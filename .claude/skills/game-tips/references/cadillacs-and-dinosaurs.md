# Cadillacs and Dinosaurs

Arcade, `dino`. Source: [ninjasrok's Guide and Walkthrough](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/arcade/575551-cadillacs-and-dinosaurs/faqs/45433); consult the character/boss section for exact tactics.

Actions: attack and jump. Together they perform an emergency special that can cost health.

Practice: reposition vertically, keep enemies on one side, and compare health lost across repeated encounters. Save before difficult encounters; keep practice saves separate from the main run.
