# Tekken 3

Arcade, `tekken3`. Source: [Kittylord's Move List](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/arcade/563192-tekken-3/faqs/980).

Notation: **1/2 = left/right punch; 3/4 = left/right kick**. These are limbs, not necessarily MAME button order. Verify physical bindings before translating combos.

Teach one character's quick attack, mid, block, and short punish before long strings. Installed MAME saves are unsupported. PlayStation training and extra modes do not apply.
