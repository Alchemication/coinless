# Final Fight

Arcade, `ffight`, two players. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fight_(video_game)); find an arcade guide before exact grab sequences or boss tactics.

Actions: attack and jump (MAME exposes a third button the game doesn't need). Attack + Jump together is a spinning attack that clears nearby enemies but costs some health.

Heroes: Haggar (grapples, spinning piledriver, strongest unarmed attack), Cody (balanced; repeated knife stabs), Guy (fast; wall jump into a flying kick). Barrels, bins and drums hold food and weapons.

Drill: reposition vertically and keep enemies on one side; compare health retained through one encounter.
