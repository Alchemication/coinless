# Heaven's Gate

Arcade, `hvnsgate`. Priority source: [GalFord's Move List and Guide](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps/572988-heavens-gate/faqs/11381). It covers PlayStation: verify arcade applicability, especially training, unlocks, and options.

Player notation: **B = Block, K = Kick, P = Punch**. Spell out directions to avoid confusing Block with Back. In GalFord's notation, **G = Guard** and **B = Back**; translate carefully. Plus means simultaneous; commas mean sequential.

- Full gauge: **B+K+P** activates Sol/Lunar power.
- While powered: **down, down+B+K+P** is the general assault.
- **B+P** is the throw combination.
